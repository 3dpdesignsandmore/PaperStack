# Privacy Policy

*(Last updated: October 28, 2026)*

PaperStack is made by 3DP Designs and More, a small business in Grand Blanc, Michigan. It was built so that the privacy story is simple enough to state in one line: **your documents never leave your device** unless you send or share them yourself.

## What we collect

Nothing. PaperStack has no accounts, no sign-ins and no servers of its own, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. We never receive your scans, the text in them, or any information about how you use the app.

## What is stored, and where

Everything the app creates — scans, exported PDFs, tags, recipients, and settings — is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. Recipients are names and addresses you type in yourself; PaperStack does not read your device's contacts.

**Device backups.** Like other apps, the app's storage is included in your device's own backups if you have them turned on: iCloud Backup on iPhone, and Google's Android backup on Android. Android limits each app's backup to a fixed size, so a large library may only be partly backed up. Those backups belong to your Apple or Google account and are governed by their policies; we have no access to them.

**Images that arrive through your device's system share sheet** are treated the same as camera scans: stored on your device, processed locally, never uploaded.

**Diagnostic log.** If you turn on diagnostic logging — a manual, opt-in setting — the app keeps a small technical log of its own activity on your device, containing no identifiers. It is never transmitted automatically; you can export it as a file yourself if you choose to share it with support, and you can clear it at any time.

**Because PaperStack has no service of its own to break into**, the security of your documents is the security of your device itself — the same lock screen and encryption that protect the rest of your phone protect your scans.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Camera** — to scan documents and receipts. Nothing is recorded except the pages you choose to keep.
- **Photos** — only when you choose to add an existing photo as a document. The app sees only the photos you pick.

PaperStack does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone or any other permission.

## Scanning and text recognition

Scanning pages and recognizing the text in them happens entirely on your device. Your images and their text are never uploaded for processing.

**On iPhone**, this is done by Apple's built-in document scanner and text-recognition engine.

**On Android**, it is done by Google's ML Kit, which runs on the device through Google Play services. ML Kit does not send your images or recognized text anywhere, but as part of how Google provides it, it may contact Google to download updates to its recognition models and send Google anonymous technical metrics about how the scanning and recognition features perform (for example, device type and how long processing took). This is collected by Google under ML Kit's terms (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms); we don't receive it.

## What is transmitted

- **Sending a document.** When you choose to send a PDF to someone, the email is handed to your device's own mail app, or the text message to your device's own messaging app. PaperStack does not operate any mail or messaging service — from that point on, whatever those apps do is governed by their own policies.
- **Sharing.** When you use the share option, the file is handed directly to your device's system share sheet.
- **App updates.** PaperStack receives updates from the App Store or Google Play, and may also download small updates directly through Expo, the service the app is built with. Checking for an update involves the usual technical details any download does — your device's network address, its platform and the app version. No information about your documents or how you use the app is included.
- **Crash reports from your device.** PaperStack itself sends no crash reports. If you have allowed your iPhone or Android device to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple or Google may pass us anonymous crash data under their own policies. You can turn that off in your device's settings.

## Deleting your data

Because there is no account and nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing for us to delete. Deleting a document in the app removes it from the app, and removing the app removes all of its data from your device. Copies you have sent, shared or saved elsewhere — and your own device backups — stay where you put them.

## Children

PaperStack collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

If a future version of PaperStack changes anything described here, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
