# Privacy Policy

*(Last updated: October 28, 2026)*

PaperStack was built so that the privacy story is simple enough to state in one line: **your documents never leave your device.**

## What we collect

Nothing. PaperStack collects no personal information, no usage statistics, no crash reports, and no analytics. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, and no servers storing anything about you.

## What is stored, and where

Everything the app creates — scans, exported PDFs, tags, recipients, and settings — is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. On iOS, that storage is included in your device's normal backups (for example, iCloud backups, if you have them enabled). Removing the app removes its data.

**Images that arrive through your device's system share sheet** are treated the same as camera scans: stored on your device, processed locally, never uploaded.

**Diagnostic log.** If you turn on diagnostic logging — a manual, opt-in setting — the app keeps a small technical log of its own activity on your device, containing no identifiers. It is never transmitted automatically; you can export it as a file yourself if you choose to share it with support, and you can clear it at any time.

**Because PaperStack has no service of its own to break into**, the security of your documents is the security of your device itself — the same lock screen and encryption that protect the rest of your phone protect your scans.

**Text recognition (OCR) runs entirely on your device.** When you use features that recognize text in your scans, the processing is performed locally by the operating system's own text-recognition engine. The text never travels anywhere for processing.

## What is transmitted

- **Sending a document.** When you choose to send a PDF to someone, the email is handed to your device's own mail app, or the text message to your device's own messaging app. PaperStack does not operate any mail or messaging service — from that point on, whatever those apps do is governed by their own policies.
- **Sharing.** When you use the share option, the file is handed directly to your device's system share sheet.
- **App updates.** If you installed PaperStack from an app store or receive app updates over the air, the update service knows that an update was downloaded, like any other app update. No information about your documents or how you use the app is included.
